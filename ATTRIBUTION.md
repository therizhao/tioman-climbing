# Attribution, sources & takedown

## This is a community compilation

The Tioman Climbing Guidebook is assembled from **many sources**, including:

- the **"Tioman Climbing Route Book"** ([PDF](https://github.com/therizhao/tioman-climbing/blob/main/dragonhorns-climb-log.pdf)) compiled by **Tam Haja**;
- the **climbers' logbook** kept at Kampung Mukut, written by many different parties over
  the years;
- **expedition and first-ascent reports** and published route notes (e.g. Cedar Wright &
  Lucho Rivera's *Alpinist* notes, David Kaszlikowski / Vertical Vision material);
- **photographs and topos** by the first-ascent teams and visiting climbers;
- direct contributions from the climbing community.

We try to **credit every author, first-ascent team, and photographer**, and to reproduce
route information faithfully to its source. Where a source is uncertain or internally
inconsistent, we say so rather than paper over it.

## If you own something shown here

If you hold copyright in any text, topo, or photograph used in this guidebook or on the
website and you want it **credited differently** or **taken down**, please contact:

**rizhaow@gmail.com**

We will respond and act promptly.
